# Style Guide

## Voice

Write like a technically trained public-account author, not like a generic AI explainer.

Use:

- Short opening paragraphs that name the issue directly.
- Specific verbs: `检出`, `迁移`, `复检`, `折算`, `暴露`, `比较`, `追溯`.
- Concrete questions a reader can understand.
- Measured judgment: `这个线索值得追查`, `还不能直接推出风险`, `这里缺的是迁移数据`.

Avoid:

- `本文将从以下几个方面展开`
- `作者主要讨论`
- `原文认为`
- `值得注意的是` repeated many times
- `不是……而是……`
- `一方面……另一方面……` when it creates a template tone
- `震惊`, `毒`, `致癌纸尿裤`, `实锤`, `翻车`, unless directly quoting and critiquing a claim

## Better Section Titles

Prefer:

- `一个检测数字，还不够`
- `总含量和实际接触，中间隔着迁移`
- `限值不能随手拿来套`
- `线索和结论之间，还差几步`
- `大数字不等于高风险`
- `家长真正需要哪些信息`

Avoid:

- `研究背景`
- `核心内容`
- `研究讨论`
- `作者主要讨论`
- `结论与启示`
- `阅读时需要注意`

## Rewriting AI-like Sentences

Replace `不是 A，而是 B` with:

- `这篇文章真正要看的，是 B。A 只是入口。`
- `这里更关键的是 B。`
- `A 可以作为线索，但 B 才决定风险判断能不能成立。`

Replace `该研究表明/指出/认为` with:

- `这组数据把问题推向了……`
- `结果里最值得看的，是……`
- `这一步的价值在于……`

Replace broad claims with evidence-chain language:

- Bad: `该产品存在严重健康风险。`
- Better: `目前能说的是：样品中检出了某物质；至于是否形成需要关注的健康风险，还缺迁移量和暴露剂量。`

## Professional Risk Language

Keep these distinctions clear:

- `危害属性`: the substance can cause adverse effects under some conditions.
- `检出`: the substance was measured in a sample.
- `总含量`: material-level amount after extraction or digestion.
- `迁移量`: amount released into a realistic contact medium.
- `暴露剂量`: amount a person may actually contact or absorb.
- `健康风险`: dose, route, duration, population, and toxicological threshold considered together.

Do not collapse these into one claim.

## Figure and Copyright Notes

When using paper screenshots, extracted figures, or adapted diagrams:

- Keep crops clean.
- Use original figure labels when needed, and add concise Chinese explanation nearby.
- Add a source note and `如有侵权，请联系删除` in the adapted/copyright statement.
